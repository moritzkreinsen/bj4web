Existing desktop dependencies, bundled examples, translations, graphics and packaging remain in their upstream locations. Its copyright and permission notice accompany the files in webide/public/codemirror/LICENSE. The host provides OpenJDK 21 and Python 3.9+ including SQLite and OpenSSL-backed scrypt. Chromium is downloaded by CI solely for browser tests, with its own bundled third-party notices. These tools are not included in application downloads.
